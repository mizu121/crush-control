NSIS
The installer engine and built-in System plugin use NSIS code. Applicable
zlib/libpng, compression-module and other original notices are reproduced in
NSIS-COPYING.txt. Windows Restart Manager is a Windows system API; our installer
queries it through System and does not ship a copy of that Windows DLL.
Source and documentation: https://nsis.sourceforge.io/
