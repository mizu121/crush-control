    Using the plugin

    Animation
    Choose Wave, Meteor, Rain or Starry in SignalRGB’s Keyboard Animation setting.
    Solid color
    Follows SignalRGB’s color and brightness across the whole keyboard.
    Rainbow lock
    Press Fn + Enter once if the board stays rainbow.

    Direct per-key RGB streaming is unavailable on stock firmware.
